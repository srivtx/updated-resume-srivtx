# srivtx.xyz

The home of **Sribatsha Dash**. It's a portfolio that behaves like a small
instrument: everything on it answers you back.

**→ [www.srivtx.xyz](https://www.srivtx.xyz)**

### Six products, six little performances

Every product has a drawn cover, and each cover's pixel mark does, in
miniature, what the product does. Point at one and watch:

- **Tulip Labs:** the tulip grows again, row by row
- **Morphotype:** a bold A melts into a thin serif A and back
- **Bionic Page:** an eye reads a line, then blinks
- **Deriva:** apps inside apps light up from the centre out
- **DeepForge:** an anvil takes a strike and throws sparks
- **Customs:** a check is stamped down, like a passport

### Writing with figures that think

The essays are about how things work, the frontier and the state of
thinking. Their figures are drawn for the essay and change as you read.

### The light switch is a tulip

It closes at night and opens by day, as real tulips do. Switching between
light and dark is never the same twice:

- 🌷 **garden:** pixel tulips sprout across the page and grow until night (or morning) arrives
- 🟩 **year:** a real year of work paints the page in contribution greens, busiest day first
- 🖍️ **read:** a sky-blue highlighter reads the words on screen, line by line
- ✨ **sky:** twilight, stars and a shooting star; by morning, a pixel sun rises

They take turns, then shuffle.

### Small things worth finding

- Select the first sentence on the home page. It burns.
- At the bottom of every page, a pixel landscape zooms in when you point at it.
